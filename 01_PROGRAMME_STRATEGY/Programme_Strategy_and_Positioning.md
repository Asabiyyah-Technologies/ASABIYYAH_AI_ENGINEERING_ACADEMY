## 1. Strategic positioning matrix

The Asabiyyah Technologies Global Applied AI Engineering Masterclass occupies a distinct strategic position in the global AI education landscape: **systems-grade applied AI engineering built for real-world resource, security, and reliability constraints**.

| Dimension | Typical Prompt / Literacy Bootcamps | University ML / Data Science Courses | Cloud Hyperscaler GenAI Tracks | **Asabiyyah Applied AI Engineering Masterclass** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary Output** | Prompt templates & notebook demos | Mathematical proofs & model training scripts | Vendor-specific cloud deployments | **Tested, evaluated, security-verified AI software products** |
| **Hardware & Cost Assumption** | Paid commercial API keys ($20–$100/mo) | University GPU clusters | Paid cloud credits & credit card verification | **8 GB–16 GB CPU laptops, open-weight GGUF models, zero-cost adapters** |
| **Evaluation Rigor** | "Vibe checks" (manual inspection) | Academic validation loss / perplexity | Vendor dashboard metrics | **Shared AI Evaluation & Observability (CD-01) + Golden Datasets** |
| **Security Baseline** | Rarely covered | Adversarial ML theory only | Cloud IAM only | **Proportionate OWASP AISVS 1.0 (CD-02) + OWASP LLM Top 10 (2025) + NDPA 2023** |
| **Connectivity Assumption** | Always-on fiber internet | Campus broadband | Cloud-only | **Online-first with Pre-Week Download Manifests & Offline Execution** |

## 2. Value proposition by stakeholder

1. **For Learners (Nigeria, Africa & International):** Acquire verifiable, portfolio-backed AI systems engineering capabilities without needing an expensive GPU or foreign credit card for commercial APIs. Graduate with four tangible engineering artefacts: a tested Common Core service, a deep specialist system, a cross-functional capstone product, and formal Evaluation (CD-01) and AISVS Security (CD-02) reports.
2. **For Employers & Technical Leads:** Hire engineers who understand `FastAPI`, `Pydantic`, hybrid retrieval (`BM25` + `RRF` + reranking), `MCP` tool interoperability, `OpenTelemetry` tracing, latency/token cost budgets, and Nigerian data protection law (`NDPA 2023`).
3. **For Asabiyyah Technologies Ltd:** Establish a flagship technical academy with modular, maintainable curriculum assets, strict quality assurance, and honest, defensible public claims.

## 3. Cohort progression and mentorship topology (PR-04)

To align the 50-learner cohort cap, 4 specialisation tracks, 1:6–7 mentor ratio, and 5 product teams of 10 (PD-05):
- **Weeks 1–4 (Common Core):** 50 learners divided into **8 Mentor Pods** (6–7 learners per pod) for foundational code reviews and readiness checks.
- **Weeks 5–10 (Specialisation):** Learners group into **Single-Track Mentor Pods** (~12–13 learners per track across the 4 tracks, split into two 6–7 learner pods per track) so specialist mentors review track-specific labs (`AAE`, `RAG`, `AGT`, `EDG`).
- **Weeks 11–14 (Integration & Capstone):** Learners re-form into **5 Cross-Functional Product Teams of ~10 learners** (each containing 2–3 specialists from each of the 4 tracks) to build, evaluate, secure, and defend an integrated AI product, while retaining individual module ownership and oral defence accountability.
